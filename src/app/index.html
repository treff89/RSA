Reading files…

  1. Anonymise. Names, IDs, dates and every other identifying tag are discarded as each file is read. Only pixels and image geometry are kept — in memory, on this device.
  2. Detect and confirm. Metal implants are found automatically. Bony landmarks are yours to place or confirm: the software says which positions it only estimated.
  3. Judge. Each measurement is shown as a number, a colour against its published range, the source of that range, and how far the evidence behind it can be trusted.

For audit, teaching and research. Not a medical device and not for diagnosis; the numbers depend on where the landmarks are put and on the quality of the view.
